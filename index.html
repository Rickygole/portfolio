Temporal OOD audit · clinical NLP

A model that scores 0.73, then zero

0.73 → 0.00
BiomedBERT F1 on post-2019 FDA drug interactions

A drug interaction classifier holding 0.7273 F1 in distribution collapsed to exactly zero on a 600-pair post-2019 out-of-distribution set, reproduced across four seeds. It did not degrade. It flatlined. A name-only in-distribution control reproduced the same 0.0000, which pins the cause on structural dependence on annotated sentence context, not drug novelty. I recovered 0.2640 F1 with KnowledgeRAG-DDI, a hybrid BM25 and FAISS retrieval probe over FDA labels with leakage-controlled indexing and reciprocal rank fusion.

IEEE ICDM 2026 · accepted

Five models, wrong in unison

19.9×
more unanimous-wrong answers than chance predicts

Across 4,089 USMLE and MedMCQA questions, five models (Llama-3-8B, Qwen-2.5-7B, Gemma-3n, GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3) agreed on a wrong answer 24.6% of the time against a 1.23% chance baseline. A distractor shuffle isolated the cause as content driven, not positional. The resulting parameter-free routing rule flags these cases at 83.0 to 96.1% precision (96.1% on MedQA, 83.0% on MedMCQA) for 6.7 to 8.0% human review cost, holding 77.5% under a held-out frontier-model substitution.

Ensembles don't average out error. They concentrate it. llm-convergent-failures
Fairness benchmark · under review, EMNLP

DART: dialect robustness in essay scoring

480 pairs
six U.S. English dialect families, plus 160 controls

A controlled benchmark validated by a three-gate pipeline (length drift, DeBERTa-v3 NLI entailment, regex feature presence) at a 92.71% pass rate. Mixed-effects regression exposed directional bias against African American English in a fine-tuned scorer (Pearson r = 0.829, QWK = 0.714) as the only family with significant negative drift (β = −0.029, p < 0.001), concentrated at the equalised-odds decision threshold. Chain-of-thought scaffolding amplified the drift across three LLM judges rather than reducing it.

Multi-omic fusion · real patient cohort

SGOF: when more data makes it worse

−0.149 → 0.012
AUROC drop under an adversarial modality: MOGONET vs. SGOF

On a real 111-patient antidepressant response cohort, adding an uninformative 258,782-SNP genotype layer dragged four fusion families below the best single modality (methylation alone, AUROC 0.692), collapsing naive concatenation to 0.334. I attributed it to uninformativeness with a synthetic noise control, then built Signal-Gated Omic Fusion, which weights modalities by held-out validation signal and held at 0.664 (paired-bootstrap change 0.012, 95% CI [−0.035, 0.059], not significant) where MOGONET dropped 0.149. The claim is stability under a bad modality, not beating the best single one.

Also on the bench

Benchmark validity

35.7% of a deep model's edge, from two features

Across 95.3M EdNet interactions, a two-feature baseline recovered 35.7% of Deep Knowledge Tracing's above-chance AUC, a construct-validity confound. I introduced the recovery ratio as a reusable diagnostic, with calibration reporting (Brier, ECE, log-loss) and multi-seed stability checks.

If a trivial baseline gets a third of your gain, the metric is the problem.
Safety-constrained RL · BIO-DQNA, second author

Unsafe actions, 12% to 2%

Clinically tiered reward functions on a custom PyTorch environment raised policy success to 94% while cutting the unsafe-action rate from 12% to 2%. Benchmarked six DQN variants (Standard, Double, Dueling, Categorical, Noisy, Rainbow) at 100 seeds each with GPU hyperparameter sweeps. Published at IEEE BIBM 2025 with J. Dacon as first author.

Quantified AI and human pedagogical divergence across 12,979 ELL sentences and introduced the Prescriptive Gap metric (t = 103.19, p < 0.001, d = 0.91). A controlled frugal-prompt intervention cut GPT-5.2 over-correction from 42.9% to 6.6%.
